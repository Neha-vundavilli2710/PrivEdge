## 1. Dataset
- 658 synthetic labeled queries across 3 classes (`cloud` / `edge` / `human`), generated by `generate_dataset.py`.
- **Labels represent this project's intended routing policy per query template, not real production traffic** - stated explicitly so results are not misread as measuring real-world usage.
- Train/test split: 458 / 200 rows, **grouped by template_id** (`GroupShuffleSplit`), so no test-set query shares a template with a training-set query.

## 7. Limitations (stated directly, not hidden)
- The 100% headline numbers in sections 2 and 4 come from a synthetic, template-generated dataset; they measure whether the router correctly learned the *intended policy*, not real-world accuracy.
- Section 6 (hand-written, out-of-distribution) is the more credible estimate of real generalization, and it is **not** 100% - e.g. both routers can over-trigger Edge AI on creative-writing prompts that merely contain the word "confidential" (`"Write a confidential-sounding spy story."`), and the ML router additionally misreads a couple of technical/definitional questions as sensitive.
- Crucially, **every miss observed moved a query toward MORE privacy protection (cloud -> edge), never toward less** - the privacy routing rate stayed at 100% across both routers and both evaluation sets. The failure mode of this system is being over-cautious, not under-cautious.
- The dataset does not cover multi-turn conversational context, non-English queries, or adversarial attempts to evade the privacy/risk detectors; these are reasonable directions for future work.
